About

Who We Are

Interlancy Education is an educational consulting and services organisation established in 2021. We work alongside educational institutions to understand their unique contexts, identify their evolving needs and support them through practical, thoughtful and collaborative solutions.

Discover Interlancy
Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

We believe every educational institution can grow stronger by understanding its people better — the educators who teach, the leaders who guide, and the students they all serve. When that understanding is real, growth follows naturally.

Our Mission

Interlancy works closely with educational institutions to understand their people and their challenges, and to help them build stronger, more sustainable ways forward — as a partner, not just a service provider.

Our Approach

What Guides Our Work

We believe meaningful educational development begins with understanding people, contexts and needs.

Child-Centred

Keeping children's learning, development and well-being at the heart of educational thinking.

Context-Driven

Understanding the unique needs and realities of every institution.

Collaborative

Working alongside educators, leaders and institutions.

Continuous

Encouraging reflection, learning and improvement.
