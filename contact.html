Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with public and private schools?

Yes, we partner with both public school districts and independent private institutions, tailoring our approach to fit the specific regulatory and operational needs of each.

How long does a typical consulting engagement last?

Our engagements range from short-term intensive workshops (1-2 weeks) to multi-year strategic transformation partnerships, depending on your institution's goals.

Are your teacher training programs accredited?

Yes, many of our professional development programs offer continuing education credits and are recognized by major educational boards.
