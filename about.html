Our Mission

To empower educational institutions globally by providing innovative, data-driven, and sustainable consulting solutions that enhance student outcomes and institutional effectiveness.

Our Vision

To be the premier partner for school transformation, where every educational institution we touch becomes a beacon of excellence, shaping the leaders of tomorrow.

Our Story

Founded over 15 years ago by a group of passionate educators and administrators, Interlancy was born out of a profound realization: schools needed a dedicated partner to navigate the rapidly changing educational landscape. What started as a small advisory group has grown into a comprehensive consultancy firm that has successfully partnered with over 500 institutions.

We believe that standard solutions do not fit the unique challenges of individual schools. That's why we emphasize a tailored approach, working closely with school leadership to design and implement strategies that resonate with their specific community and goals.
